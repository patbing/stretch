Privacy — STRETCH

STRETCH is a personal reading aid. The minimum possible information leaves your browser: the phrase you selected, and a little context around it.

What is sent to Pollinations

One request per stretch you ask for, whose body is exactly:

FieldWhat it holds
selectionthe phrase you selected (1–600 characters)
beforeup to 1000 characters before it, same paragraph
afterup to 1000 characters after it, same paragraph

Never sent: the page URL, page HTML, cookies, other tabs, browsing history, your account email, or any identifier. There is no telemetry and no error reporting.

Where your key lives

Your Pollinations sk_… key is stored in chrome.storage.local — your browser profile's local extension storage. It is protected from ordinary page JavaScript, but it is not encrypted at rest. Only the service worker can read it; the content script never receives it, and it never appears in the page, a URL, or a log.

Authorized keys normally expire after 7 days and can be revoked sooner. Disconnect in the popup clears the local key; revoking it at Pollinations invalidates it everywhere.

What the site can see

A generated addition is inserted into the page, so that site can read it and can remove it. Shadow DOM is not a confidentiality boundary. Nothing else you read is shared.

The only network requests STRETCH makes

  • POST https://enter.pollinations.ai/api/device/code and …/device/token — sign-in only.
  • GET https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/models — prices and tier eligibility.
  • POST https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions — one per stretch you asked for.
